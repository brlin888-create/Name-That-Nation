Africa - Continent
Country: Nigeria
What it is famous for: Being Africa's most populous country, Nollywood (film industry), and vibrant music like Afrobeats.
Continent: Africa
Languages Spoken: English (official), Hausa, Yoruba, Igbo, and over 500 indigenous languages.
Famous Landmarks: Zuma Rock, Aso Rock, Olumo Rock.
Culture Description: Rich in music, dance, literature, and colorful festivals like the Eyo Festival.
Geography: Rainforests in the south, savannas in the north, and river systems like the Niger and Benue.
Other: Known as the "Giant of Africa" for its economy and population.

Country: Egypt
What it is famous for: Ancient pyramids, pharaohs, and the Nile River.
Continent: Africa
Languages Spoken: Arabic (official), English and French widely understood.
Famous Landmarks: Pyramids of Giza, Sphinx, Karnak Temple.
Culture Description: Deeply tied to ancient civilizations, Islam, and Mediterranean influences.
Geography: Sahara Desert with the Nile cutting through.
Other: Called the "Gift of the Nile."

Country: South Africa
What it is famous for: Nelson Mandela, diverse ecosystems, and beautiful coastlines.
Continent: Africa
Languages Spoken: 11 official languages including Zulu, Xhosa, Afrikaans, and English.
Famous Landmarks: Table Mountain, Robben Island, Kruger National Park.
Culture Description: A blend of African, European, and Asian traditions.
Geography: Deserts, grasslands, coastal regions.
Other: Nicknamed the "Rainbow Nation."

Country: Kenya
What it is famous for: World-class safaris and long-distance runners.
Continent: Africa
Languages Spoken: Swahili and English (official).
Famous Landmarks: Maasai Mara Reserve, Mount Kenya, Lake Nakuru.
Culture Description: Strong Maasai and Swahili traditions, colorful attire.
Geography: Savannas, mountains, lakes.
Other: Home to the Great Migration.

Country: Morocco
What it is famous for: Ancient cities, souks, and the Atlas Mountains.
Continent: Africa
Languages Spoken: Arabic (official), Berber, French.
Famous Landmarks: Hassan II Mosque, Chefchaouen, Atlas Mountains.
Culture Description: Arab, Berber, and European influences.
Geography: Coastal plains, rugged mountains, desert.
Other: Known for historic medinas and crafts.

Other: Famous filming location for "The Lord of the Rings."

Antarctica - Continent
Continent: Antarctica
What it is famous for: Being the coldest, driest, and windiest continent.
Languages Spoken: No official language; research stations use English, Russian, Spanish, and others.
Famous Landmarks: South Pole, McMurdo Station, Antarctic Peninsula.
Culture Description: No indigenous culture; primarily scientists and researchers.
Geography: Ice sheets, glaciers, and mountains.
Other: Antarctica is not a country but a continent governed by an international treaty for peaceful research.
