Africa - Continent
Country: Nigeria
What it is famous for: Oil, Nollywood (film industry), and diverse cultures.
Continent: Africa
Languages Spoken: English (official), Hausa, Yoruba, Igbo, and over 500 other languages.
Famous Landmarks: Zuma Rock, Ogbunike Caves, Erin Ijesha Waterfall.
Culture Description: A blend of ancient traditions and modern culture, with rich art, music, and cuisine.
Geography: Tropical rainforests, savannas, and the Sahara Desert in the north.
Other: Most populous country in Africa.
